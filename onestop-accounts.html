How the numbers are worked out
Total cash in = Restaurant cash + Room cash
Closing cash = Opening + Total cash in − Cash out
Opening = the closing figure of the line above, carried across days
Difference = Physical cash − Closing cash
UPI is collected and spent outside the cash box, so it never moves the closing figure.
Self cash is the same — it counts as expenditure, but it is not taken out of the box.
